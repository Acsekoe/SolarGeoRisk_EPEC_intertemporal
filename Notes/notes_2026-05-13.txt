State-of-the-art gap framing — refined.

Initial framing was "no strategic PV supply chain models." This is too strong.
The bib already contains a firm-level game-theoretic PV literature strand
(chen2017dual_pv, chensu2017dual_pv_welfare, zhao2023pv_subsidy,
zhao2023pv_industrial, kharaji2019solar_scsc, chen2020pv_trade) that
formulates two integrated supply chains as Stackelberg or Cournot competitors
under government subsidy. The defensible contribution claim is therefore
narrower and more precise: no existing work models the global PV module
supply chain as a multi-region EPEC with simultaneous endogenous bilateral
strategic price offers and intertemporal capacity decisions. The distinction
is (a) multi-region (>2 strategic players), (b) endogenous prices on
bilateral export routes rather than a single market-clearing price between
two chains, and (c) intertemporal capacity coupled to those price decisions.
All three together are absent from existing PV strategic-game literature.

Implication for paper rhetoric: SoTA Para 1 must explicitly acknowledge the
firm-level game-theoretic PV strand as a "separate strand" rather than
omitting it, otherwise reviewers familiar with that literature will reject
the gap claim.
