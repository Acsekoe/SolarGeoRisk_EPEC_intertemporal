Literature search result: Shum (2019, "Heliopolitics", Energy Strategy Reviews) is a strong, peer-reviewed citation explicitly framing PV trade protectionism through a "Solar Trilemma" and arguing that Sections 201/301 tariffs counteract decarbonization progress and risk a return to 1970s/1980s "managed trade" — useful motivation for why strategic-interaction (EPEC) modelling of PV trade is warranted rather than treating trade frictions as exogenous shocks.

Erol et al. (2024, Operations Management Research) provides the complementary empirical-methodological angle: SPvESCs are global networks vulnerable to disruption with sensing/seizing of new business models and contingency planning identified as the dominant resilience enablers — supports the claim that existing PV supply-chain analyses do not endogenize strategic responses to disruption, motivating an EPEC approach.

Chen and Su (2019, Renewable Energy, "International competition and trade conflict in a dual photovoltaic supply chain system") is the most direct game-theoretic precedent — a Stackelberg/Cournot-style dual-PV-supply-chain model showing that trade protection reduces total welfare and demand, while free trade dominates; useful methodological precedent to cite when justifying the EPEC modelling choice, not just the motivation sentence.

Convergence-scheme novelty check: Franci, Fabiani, Schmidt & Staudigl (2024/2025, arXiv:2404.02605, ECC 2025) propose a *proximal Gauss-Seidel* method for multi-leader-multi-follower games with explicit adaptive (monotonically decreasing) regularization parameter tau(k+1) <= tau(k), citing Facchinei-Piccialli-Sciandrone (COAP 2011) as the original source of adaptive proximal regularization in decomposition for generalized potential games. This is the closest precedent to our scheduled c_k. The original Hori-Fukushima (JOTA 2018/2019) method appears to use a fixed penalty technique (no scheduled adaptation), and Franci et al. position their 2024 algorithm explicitly as an extension to multi-leader settings of their prior single-level Gauss-Seidel work. Our specific combination — scheduled damping omega_k AND scheduled proximal weight c_k inside a sequential MPEC diagonalization for EPECs — does not appear to have a direct precedent in the surveyed literature, but the *adaptive proximal weight alone* is clearly not new. Novelty claim in the paper should therefore be softened to emphasize the joint omega_k + c_k schedule and the EPEC/MPEC setting, citing Facchinei-Piccialli-Sciandrone 2011 and Franci-Fabiani-Schmidt-Staudigl 2024 as the closest related work.
