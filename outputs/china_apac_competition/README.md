These figures use the same regional colors, serif type, white bar dividers, and compact legend style as the manuscript's `manufacturing_capacity_pathway` plots.

Figures remain in this folder; their supporting CSV tables are in [`csv/`](csv/).

For an active route, the model's market-price relation, to the figures' displayed precision, is

`market price ≈ supplier offer + shipping + mu_offer`.

`mu_offer` is the dual on the constraint that the exporter's total shipments cannot exceed its chosen offer quantity. It is **not a separately paid charge or a direct shadow price on installed physical capacity**. The strategic offer wedge is `supplier offer - manufacturing cost`; it can be negative. The combined supplier blocks come from one equilibrium so that their offers, flows, duals, and prices explain the same market outcome. The 27-profile mean accounting is a supplementary summary rather than a synthetic equilibrium.

The model pins each domestic offer to its period-specific unit manufacturing cost (`p_offer[r,r,t] = c_man_t[r,t]`), and manufacturing output has this linear unit cost in the producer objective. The merit-order figures use that value as manufacturing marginal cost. The width and ordering show the **observed cleared allocation** in each selected equilibrium. They do not show the suppliers' unused capacity or a counterfactual competitive supply curve. The selected equilibrium can differ across destination-period panels.

The all-period median figure selects a complete equilibrium *separately for each destination and period*. It does not take independent medians of each price component: those medians can fail to add to the median market price (by up to about 34 USD/kW in these outputs). It also does not imply the 24 plotted bars all come from one global equilibrium.

Underlying values are in `csv/market_price_accounting_profiles.csv`, `csv/market_price_accounting_mean.csv`, and `csv/normalized_supplier_price_components_C20.csv`.

Each figure is also available as a PNG. The CSV files contain the underlying plotted values. The figures are descriptive computational outcomes; the 27 profiles are not independent samples or necessarily distinct global equilibria.
