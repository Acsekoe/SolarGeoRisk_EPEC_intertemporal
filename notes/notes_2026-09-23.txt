[Results / equilibrium properties] Clean-objective Stage-2 factorial (outputs/clean_stage2_factorial_20260923_123037): with the full market-price producer margin and no quadratic/proximal penalties, 28 of 36 branches pass the one-start 1% unilateral-deviation test (7 no-pass, 1 solver failure), versus 16 of 36 under the previous objective. The EU/US manufacturing exit is not robust to this change: 2040 EU capacity is 28-61 GW (median 55) and US 5-109 GW (median 75); only one branch shows near-exit of the US and none of the EU.

[Results] Robust across all 28 accepted outcomes: China remains the largest manufacturer (336-590 GW in 2040), both China and ROW shed most of their 2025 overcapacity by 2030 (system capacity/demand ratio falls from 2.55 to about 1.15), Africa grows to its expansion bound (~10.9 GW), and import-region prices stay well above Chinese/APAC prices. The 2040 Chinese price is identical in all outcomes (86.65 USD/kW), i.e. pinned at domestic marginal cost, so price dispersion comes entirely from export-offer wedges into other regions.

[Methodology / convergence] Update order has little effect on the pass rate in the clean factorial (CH-first 10/12, AF-first 9/12, EU-first 9/12), so the earlier "place China first" heuristic is not supported. Search settings mainly shift which outcome is selected: the price-factor-1.2 initializations cluster in a high-China-capacity, low-EU/US family, and damping 0.4 versus 0.3 lowers median 2040 total capacity by about 7% in matched pairs.
