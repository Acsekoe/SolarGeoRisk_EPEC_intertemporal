The exact accepted iteration-21 paper profile is not a zero-proximal computational 1%-equilibrium. A frozen common-profile audit with incumbent, standard zero-change/cost-price, and perturbed starts finds a maximum relative unilateral gain of 47.58% for ROW; CH, APAC, US, and AF also exceed 1%, while EU is at 0.85%.

Clean zero-proximal Gauss-Seidel with alpha=0.65 does not converge to a solver-verified 1%-equilibrium from the paper profile or seeded +/-5% and +/-10% full-strategy perturbations. The instability concentrates in CH, ROW, and APAC; the mild 25% cost-price interpolation develops a detected two-cycle led by APAC, while US, EU, and AF are usually nearly satisfied after the first few sweeps.

The best locally initialized profile arose from the 50% paper-price/50% manufacturing-cost interpolation with alpha=0.50 after six sweeps, but its frozen audit still gives 3.84% for CH and 1.54% for APAC. Reducing damping to 0.30 merely delays a large response-branch transition, so the previously certified 0.661% cost-price candidate remains a distinct robustness result rather than the limit of the defensible local search around the paper solution.
