# Workflow summary: basin-guided equilibrium search

Created: 2026-09-17 20:05:52 Europe/Vienna

## Current methodological direction

Reviewer 2 asks for an approach that deliberately triggers multiple different
equilibria rather than selecting one result from a small number of runs. The
proposed response is a **basin-guided search with independent equilibrium
verification**. Numerical stabilization is used to locate diverse promising
regions of the non-convex strategy space; equilibrium status is never inferred
from small damped movements alone.

1. **Stage 0 -- primitive cold initialization.** Construct the state only from
   corrected model primitives: observed 2025 capacity, zero net-capacity
   changes, true-demand bids, and bilateral offers equal to each exporter's
   2025 manufacturing cost. No historical equilibrium is loaded.
2. **Stage 1 -- stabilized basin discovery.** Run proximally regularized,
   adaptively damped Gauss--Seidel searches under three player orders. Retain
   the last complete solver-clean checkpoint for each order as a basin anchor.
   These anchors are not called equilibria.
3. **Stage 2 -- de-regularized refinement and diversification.** Cross each
   anchor with two period-specific bilateral-offer initializations
   (`pf100`: manufacturing cost; `pf120`: 1.20 times manufacturing cost), two
   Stage-1 capacity-path weights (0.50 and 1.00), and two fixed damping values
   (0.30 and 0.40). This gives 24 branches. Stage 2 uses zero proximal
   penalties, no move cap, no gain filter, and no player freezing.
4. **Independent verification.** Audit the initialization and every complete
   sweep against one common frozen profile. For each region, solve an
   unregularized unilateral best response and compute the normalized welfare
   gain. A profile passes the declared local 1% criterion only if all six solves
   succeed and the maximum relative gain is at most 1%.

This structure directly addresses equilibrium multiplicity: player order,
offer initialization, capacity initialization, and damping intentionally probe
different attraction basins, while the acceptance test is independent of
those search parameters.

## Present evidence and claim boundary

- Fifteen of the 24 branches produced numerically distinct profiles passing
  the one-start local 1% audit; accepted gains range from 0.209492% to
  0.995711%.
- A subsequent three-start frozen-profile audit completed all 270 optimizer
  attempts successfully, but none remained below 1%; three-start gains range
  from 1.602905% to 5.451140%.
- The 15 profiles must therefore be described as **distinct one-start local
  1%-equilibrium candidates**, not multistart-robust or globally certified Nash
  equilibria.
- The defensible methodological contribution is the separation of stabilized
  basin discovery, zero-proximal refinement, and frozen-profile regret
  verification.

The economic horizon is 2025--2040. The recorded 2045 capacity is only a
post-horizon terminal stock used for salvage valuation; there is no 2045
market, demand, trade, price, or operating payoff.

## Economic pattern across candidates

Despite price multiplicity, all candidates produce a coherent structural
story: China remains the largest producer after substantial capacity
retirement, APAC expands in every profile and becomes the principal secondary
exporter, and EU and US manufacturing becomes economically negligible by
2030. Cost-level Stage-2 initialization (`pf100`) consistently reaches a
central declining-price family, whereas `pf120` accesses additional low-price
and intertemporally non-monotonic basins. Initialization therefore acts as a
basin-selection device rather than mechanically determining the final price.
