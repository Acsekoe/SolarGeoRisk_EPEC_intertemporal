# Three corrected-demand equilibria found

Created: 2026-09-16 23:21:46 Europe/Vienna

## Finding

The project now has three distinct corrected-demand profiles satisfying the
declared one-start local 1%-equilibrium criterion. Each accepted profile was
checked by a common-frozen-profile audit in which all six players solved an
unrestricted unilateral best-response problem with zero proximal penalties.
All 18 audit solves succeeded.

| ID | Construction | Accepted result |
|---|---|---:|
| E1 | Stage-1 iteration 23; cost-price reset; CH--AF--APAC--EU--ROW--US; unpenalized all-player Gauss--Seidel with \(\alpha=0.30\) | sweep 6, 0.621395% CH |
| E2 | Stage-1 iteration 24; 0.50 capacity-path weight; offers at 1.20 times cost; CH--ROW--APAC--US--EU--AF; unpenalized all-player Gauss--Seidel with \(\alpha=0.30\) | sweep 5, 0.835510% APAC |
| E3 | Stage-1 iteration 24; full capacity path; offers at 1.20 times cost; CH--ROW--APAC--US--EU--AF; unpenalized all-player Gauss--Seidel with \(\alpha=0.30\) | sweep 7, 0.910692% APAC |

All three generation paths used zero algorithmic proximal penalties, no move
cap, no gain filter, no player freezing, and sequential updates of all players.
The initialization factors only selected starting profiles and did not restrict
the subsequent best responses or the final audit.

E2 and E3 are not numerical duplicates: their maximum absolute difference is
9.303312 in net capacity change, 9.078213 in bilateral offers, and 8.066464 in
clearing prices. Both also differ materially from E1. Corrected demand differs
only at roughly \(10^{-8}\), so the equilibrium multiplicity is expressed in
strategies, capacities, prices, trade, and payoffs rather than demand quantity.

## Factorial-run outcome

The 24-branch corrected Stage-1 factorial completed at 2026-09-16 21:36:39.
It crossed three Stage-1 anchors with price factors 1.0/1.2, capacity-path
weights 0.5/1.0, and damping 0.30/0.40. Two branches were accepted, 21 finished
without passing, and one failed during China's sweep-12 best-response solve.
The common successful setting was the iteration-24 anchor, offers at 1.20 times
manufacturing cost, the CH--ROW--APAC--US--EU--AF order, and \(\alpha=0.30\).

The proposed warm-start recovery experiment is no longer needed to reach the
minimum of three equilibria and has not been launched.

## Caveats and next direction

These are one-start computational local 1%-equilibria, not globally certified
or exact Nash equilibria. E2 and E3 share one Stage-1 order, and E1's generation
was not reproduced by the nominal matched branch because the nonconvex first
China best response selected a different local solution after microscopic
initialization differences. Next, run a separately disclosed multistart audit
of E1--E3 and compare their objectives, capacities, offers, prices, and trade
flows for the Reviewer 2 response.
