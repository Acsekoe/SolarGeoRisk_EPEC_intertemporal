## Decommissioning finding

The corrected profiles let EU and US decommission their entire starting capacity during 2025-2030. The explicit decommission-rate constraint itself is not binding.

Implemented constraints are `Kcap[t+1] = Kcap[t] + 5*(Icap_pos[t] - Dcap_neg[t])`, nonnegative `Kcap`, and `Dcap_neg[t] <= g_dec[t]*Kcap[t]`. The corrected workbook sets `g_dec=1/yr` for every region. With no simultaneous investment, nonnegative next-period capacity gives the tighter effective bound `Dcap_neg <= Kcap/5` GW/yr. EU therefore retires at 4.4 GW/yr from 22 GW, and the US at 8.4 GW/yr from 42 GW, reaching approximately zero in 2030. They use about 20% of the explicit `g_dec*Kcap` ceiling but essentially 100% of the effective nonnegativity bound.

The manuscript equation in `IEEE Paper/SolarGeoRisk_epec_tracked_version_r1.tex` currently states `Delta K >= g_dec`, which has the wrong sign and omits the capacity multiplier. It should use the implemented gross formulation (`0 <= Dcap_neg <= g_dec*Kcap`, `Delta K = Icap_pos-Dcap_neg`) or the simplified net lower bound `Delta K >= -g_dec*Kcap`, while retaining nonnegative capacity.
