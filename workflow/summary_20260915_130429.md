# Research summary — 2026-09-15 13:04 CEST

## Current status

All three update sequences reported as converged in the original manuscript now have replacement profiles that satisfy the agreed **computational local 1%-equilibrium criterion**. For each candidate, all rivals are held at one common frozen profile, algorithmic proximal penalties are zero, and one unilateral deviation problem is solved for every player from that player's candidate strategy. A profile passes when all six solves succeed and the maximum nonnegative relative payoff improvement is at most 1%.

This is a local computational criterion, not a proof of a global Nash equilibrium, uniqueness, or global optimality. Three-start results are retained as stronger diagnostics but are not the agreed acceptance test.

## Results by manuscript update sequence

### Equilibrium 1: CH → AF → APAC → EU → ROW → US

- The original manuscript profile was reconstructed at iteration 24, the first endpoint of three consecutive reported strategy-change residuals below 1%. Its replay error was `2.22e-16`.
- The original frozen-profile audit failed strongly: maximum one-start relative gain `405.505%` for AF.
- The successful repair used a 50% interpolation from the manuscript offer prices toward manufacturing cost, followed by six zero-proximal Gauss–Seidel sweeps with `alpha=0.50`.
- The accepted Branch-F sweep-6 profile has maximum one-start frozen gain `0.7969%` for APAC. All six solves succeeded. Other gains are CH `0.5827%`, AF `0.0090%`, EU `0.0010%`, ROW `0.7048%`, and US `0.7373%`.
- The separate three-start diagnostic found `2.5722%` for CH and `1.4939%` for APAC. Not every auxiliary multistart attempt succeeded, although every selected player solution succeeded. The diagnostic therefore does not pass 1%.
- Package: `outputs/equilibria/ch-af-apac-eu-row-us/branch_f_sweep_006_20260915/`

### Equilibrium 2: CH → AF → EU → US → ROW → APAC

- The original manuscript profile was reconstructed at iteration 15, the first endpoint of three consecutive reported strategy-change residuals below 1%. Its replay error was `2.58e-16`.
- The original frozen-profile audit failed strongly: maximum one-start relative gain `258.441%` for AF.
- The Branch-F sweep-6 handoff remained above tolerance at `3.063%`. The analogous adaptive O6 continuation approached the threshold at sweep 10 (`1.392%`) but subsequently moved away from it: `2.196%` at sweep 15, `4.799%` at sweep 20, `3.924%` at sweep 25, and `4.592%` at sweep 30.
- Refinement therefore branched from O6 sweep 10. Players already within 1% were frozen, while remaining profitable deviators received zero-proximal responses with `alpha=0.10` and normalized applied moves capped at `0.01`.
- After three selective sweeps, the accepted profile has maximum one-start frozen gain `0.9189%` for CH. All six solves succeeded. Other gains are AF `0.0003%`, EU effectively `0%`, US `0.4435%`, ROW `0.0222%`, and APAC `0.7538%`.
- The separate three-start diagnostic found `4.0403%` for CH and `2.0933%` for APAC, with all attempts successful. The diagnostic does not pass 1%.
- Package: `outputs/equilibria/ch-af-eu-us-row-apac/o6_s010_selective_s003_20260915/`
